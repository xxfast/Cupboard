Cupboard — app icon directions

Six minimalist reductions of the CuP mark: cup, saucer, steam, play triangle. Each keeps the warm gradient body against a plum ink, and is drawn to survive at 16 px. Reference the id in chat to go deeper on one.

1a Cup & Play
44 / 30 / 18

The literal translation: half-disc bowl, hooked handle, saucer bar, play knocked out of the body.

1b Negative Disc
44 / 30 / 18

One solid coin, cup cut out of it. Strongest silhouette of the set and the most forgiving at tiny sizes.

1c Slide → Cup
44 / 30 / 18

A stacked 16:9 slide whose bottom edge bows into a shallow bowl — no handle, no dome; it reads as a deck first.

1d Monogram C
44 / 30 / 18

C for Cupboard, play in the aperture, saucer as the baseline. Reads as a brand letter, not a beverage.

1e Top-Down
44 / 30 / 18

Looking down into the cup: rim ring, gradient surface, play cut into it. Concentric and very stable.

1f Line & Steam
44 / 30 / 18

All stroke, no fill — steam as three risers. The one that also works as a 1 px toolbar glyph.
